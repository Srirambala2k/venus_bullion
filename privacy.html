Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

Venus Bullion Gold Trading L.L.C (“Venus Bullion”, “we”) is a trading company registered in the United Arab Emirates, Register No. 2918727, at No. 203, Al Ghanim Building, Al Dhagaya Gold Souq, Deira, Dubai, U.A.E.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us through the contact form or by calling or messaging us: your name, company, phone number or email address, the area you are interested in, and any message you write.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to carry out the documentation checks that apply to commercial trades.
  • We do not sell your details or use them for advertising.

How the form works

Depending on how the site is configured, submitting the form either opens WhatsApp with your enquiry pre-filled, so the message is sent by you through WhatsApp, or sends it to our enquiry inbox through a form service. WhatsApp and any form service act under their own privacy terms.

Cookies and local storage

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Your browser stores two small preferences on your own device: the language you selected and whether you have already seen the welcome animation. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts, which delivers the typefaces used on this site and may receive your IP address when fonts load.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), if you choose to contact us through it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to deal with your request and to meet record-keeping duties that apply to our business.

Your choices

You may ask us what details we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete them, or object to our using them, by contacting us at +971 50 126 3566.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
