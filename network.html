PARTNERS
01 — Sourcing

Global Suppliers

Venus Bullion works with commercial counterparties who can evidence where their material comes from. Every relationship starts with documentation, not just price.

  • Source-of-origin documentation requested
  • Responsible-sourcing expectations for every supplier
  • Periodic review of counterparties
  • Multi-market sourcing relationships
02 — Refining

Refining Partners

Material is processed through approved refining partners, with purity and certification confirmed before a trade is completed. Named partners are published once verified.

  • Approved partners, listed once confirmed
  • Stated purity grades per transaction
  • Independent assay and certification where required
  • Documented handover from refinery
03 — Movement

Logistics Partners

Precious goods move through secure, trackable channels. Ground, air and vault-to-vault transfers are arranged with approved providers for each transaction.

  • Secure ground and air transfer
  • Shipment tracking from dispatch to delivery
  • Insurance arranged per transaction
  • Full chain-of-custody paperwork
04 — Storage

Vaulting Partners

Where storage is required, Venus Bullion arranges secure vaulting with approved facilities, with clear records of what is held and where.

  • Secure, approved vaulting facilities
  • Allocated and unallocated options, where offered
  • Independent audit and clear records
  • Round-the-clock security monitoring
